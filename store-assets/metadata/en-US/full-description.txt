Identify a plant from a photo — and explore far beyond plants.

NatureLens helps identify plants, trees, crops, insects, mushrooms, birds, and fish from photos. It can also recognize nature sounds. Submit up to three angles to give the analysis more context and turn every encounter into useful knowledge.

IDENTIFY WITH EVIDENCE
See common and scientific names, result confidence, observed traits, similar alternatives, reference images, and available sources. If the evidence is not strong enough, the app asks for another capture instead of inventing certainty.

PROTECT PEOPLE AND PETS
Toxicity and danger alerts are highlighted without hiding important details. When reliable data is available, NatureLens shows risks to people, dogs, and cats and helps you recognize look-alike species. Image identification can be wrong: never eat, touch, or treat a species as safe based only on the app.

CARE AND KEEP TRACK
Automatically save discoveries, organize your collection, and record nicknames, places, notes, photos, and moments. For plants, explore light, soil, water, propagation, common problems, seasonal schedules, and reminders based on actions you actually confirm.

FROM BASICS TO AGRONOMY
Learn visually and go deeper at your own pace. When the species, crop, region, and sources support it, the app presents fertilizer tables, crop stages, climate context, safety, and Integrated Pest Management. Numeric guidance appears only when there is an adequate basis; missing data never becomes invented advice.

ONE LENS FOR NATURE
Each category has its own dossier. Insects may include life cycle, diet, habitat, ecological role, and IPM when relevant. Birds and fish prioritize identification, habitat, distribution, and conservation. Mushrooms use conservative warnings. Sound results preserve recording context, alternatives, and evidence.

Explore facts, guides, your collection, journal, check-ins, and community in 17 languages.
