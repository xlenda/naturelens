Discover what is in front of your camera and turn every encounter with nature into useful knowledge.

NatureLens uses artificial intelligence to help identify plants, insects, mushrooms, birds, and fish from photos. It can also analyze visible signs in crops. You can submit up to three angles to give each identification more context.

IDENTIFY WITH CONTEXT
See the common and scientific name, confidence level, and available details for each result. When verified data exists, the app can also show distribution, monthly records, characteristics, and sources for further research.

SAFETY COMES FIRST
Important toxicity, danger, and look-alike warnings are shown in full and highlighted. Sections without reliable data are simply omitted. Image identification can be wrong: never eat, handle, or treat a species as safe based only on an app result.

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
Save discoveries, add nicknames, organize them by room, and write observations. For plants, track soil checks and watering events that you explicitly record. A timeline keeps the essential history of each saved item.

LEARN AS YOU EXPLORE
Browse daily facts, topic guides, plant care conditions, and ecological information suited to each category. NatureLens is available in 17 languages.

NatureLens is an educational tool. It does not replace specialists, health professionals, agronomists, or environmental authorities.
